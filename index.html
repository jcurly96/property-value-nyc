Signs Your Property May Have Development Potential

The dirt beneath your building may be worth more than the building itself. These are the most common signals we look for.

  • ✓ Your building is 2–4 stories in a zone that permits 8–20 stories
  • ✓ The neighborhood has seen substantial new construction nearby
  • ✓ Your building is mostly commercial or office space (467-m territory)
  • ✓ Tenants are market-rate and on short-term leases
  • ✓ Income value has been flat while nearby land values have appreciated
  • ✓ Your lot is a corner, oversized, or positioned for assemblage
  • ✓ No air rights have been transferred from the property
  • ✓ Developers have approached you unsolicited about the property
